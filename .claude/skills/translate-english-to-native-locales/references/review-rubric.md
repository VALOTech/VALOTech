# Multidimensional review rubric
A critical failure is not offset by strengths elsewhere. Do not reduce the review to one overall score.

```yaml
review:
  factual_accuracy:
  protected_meaning:
  reasoning_and_claims:
  evidence_and_qualifications:
  claim_currency:
  information_structure:
  paragraph_purpose_and_progression:
  sentence_clarity_and_cohesion:
  native_idiomaticity:
  locale_and_market_fit:
  genre_and_register_fit:
  terminology_consistency:
  capitalization_and_style_consistency:
  surface_and_length_fit:
  technical_integrity:
```

When alternatives exist, use blind pairwise comparison. The reviewer first identifies issues, then revises. For native-language quality, use reviewers from the relevant target market and domain. For high-risk legal content, add a qualified legal reviewer.
