# Surface profiles

## Slides
- Use one principal message per slide area.
- Prefer takeaway titles when the deck style permits.
- Keep peer bullets parallel.
- Prioritize content rather than shrinking text.
- Do not turn uncertain evidence into a confident slogan.

## Reports and proposals
- Lead with the finding, proposition, or decision.
- Connect evidence to implication.
- Distinguish fact, interpretation, recommendation, and action.
- Keep qualifications material and recommendations actionable.

## Speaker notes
- Use a natural spoken register suited to the audience.
- A useful sequence is opening message, explanation, example, and transition.
- Do not repeat every word on the slide.

## HTML and product UI
- Identify the component role: heading, CTA, status, helper text, tooltip, empty state, or error.
- Optimize for scanning, action, and available space.
- Error messages should state what happened and the next useful action when known.
- Do not blame the user.

## Marketing, hero, and tagline copy
- Compression is the work: a hero line or tagline earns attention by saying one true thing sharply, not by explaining the mechanism. Leave the full explanation to body copy.
- Prefer a concrete, category-true line over a received trope ("more connected than ever, yet lonelier", "unlock a world of possibilities").
- Never buy punch with fidelity: do not inflate, add, or drop a claim to make the line land. If the sharpest true line still over- or under-states the source, choose the honest one and note the gap.

## Documentation and code text
- Preserve identifiers, APIs, enums, placeholders, markup, code fences, and syntax.
- Keep comments purposeful and concise.
- Prefer recognized technical terms over invented paraphrases.

## Legal, regulatory, policy, and control content
- Preserve actors, obligations, permissions, prohibitions, exceptions, conditions, scope, certainty, and legal effect.
- Do not improve naturalness by weakening or strengthening a requirement.
- If a plain-language rewrite could change effect, stay closer to the source and flag the issue.
