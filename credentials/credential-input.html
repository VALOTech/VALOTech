DATABASE_URL

Everything the investor room stores. Locally this is what make infra-up starts: Postgres on 5434, not 5432 — VALO Compliance holds 5432 and VALO Ads holds 5433, and a stack that binds a port another already has either fails or silently reaches the wrong database.

SESSION_SECRET

Signs the session cookie. At least 32 bytes of randomness. Rotating it signs every live session out, which is the intended emergency lever and not a thing to do casually.

Mail carrierblocked

What carries a message to an investor is unanswered — see the register, MAIL-DEC-01. Until it is answered no mail is sent, the invitation flow is blocked, and the gateway's contact path stays a mailto: link that opens the reader's own client. The key lands here with the answer.
