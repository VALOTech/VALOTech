Cookies and what is stored in your browser

What VALO Tech writes to your browser — on this site and in the investor hall — when, and why. The list is short, and only one thing on it is ever sent back to us.

Last updated 2026-09-16

What can be stored

What When it is set Category
A note that the opening animation has played When you first arrive, unless your system asks for reduced motion Necessary
Language preference When you choose a language Necessary
A note that you opened the investor chapters When you open them on the home page Necessary
Session cookie After you sign in to the investor hall Necessary

The three categories

Every VALO site uses the same three, so the posture you meet on one of them is the posture on all of them.

Necessary On

Everything in the list above is in this category and nothing else is. None of it can be switched off without breaking what it does. Three of the four stay in your browser and identify nobody; the session cookie is sent back with every request, because that is how a cookie works and how the hall knows which reader is asking.

Analytics Off

Anything that counts, measures or attributes a visit belongs here. Nothing on this site is in this category today: there is no analytics to turn on.

Marketing Off

Anything that follows you from one site to another belongs here. The category is carried because the rest of the ecosystem carries it, and it is expected to stay empty.

Nothing optional is loaded

Not loaded and then switched off — not loaded at all. A script that arrives and afterwards checks a setting has already made its request, and the request is the part you would have been agreeing to.

Change your answer

This is the same choice the banner asks for, and it is stored in your browser alone. Changing it takes effect on your next page load.

Clearing what is there

Your browser's own site-data control removes all of it. The animation note and the investor-chapter note last only until you close your browser; signing out of the investor hall ends the session cookie; and choosing a language again replaces the language preference.

What the investor hall records is not this

Signed in, the hall records which documents you have opened, so it can show you what is new since you were last there. That is an account record about a named person rather than a cookie or a measurement, and the hall's own privacy notice covers it.
