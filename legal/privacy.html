Privacy on this site

This page is about valotech.org, the public site you are reading: what reaches us when you visit, what is written to your browser, and where to write to us.

Last updated 2026-09-15

What a visit leaves

Nothing on this page reports your visit to us. One thing may be written to your browser when you arrive — a note that the opening animation has played, so it does not play again while your browser is open; if your system asks for reduced motion the animation never runs and the note is never written. Delivering a page to you necessarily discloses your network address to whoever serves it, which is true of every website; we do not gather those records and we build nothing from them.

What we measure

Nothing. There is no analytics on this site, and no script here counts, times or attributes a visit. If that ever changes it will sit behind a choice you make, and nothing will load until you have made it.

What is stored in your browser

Four things at most: the note about the opening animation, the language you chose, the fact that you opened the investor chapters on the home page, and the session that signing in gives you. The first three never leave your browser and identify nobody. The fourth is a cookie, and being sent back is what a cookie is for: it travels with every request you make while signed in, because that is how the hall knows which reader is asking. The cookies page lists all four and says when each is set.

If you write to us

Your message and your e-mail address reach our mailbox and stay there while the conversation is still useful. Nothing you send is used for anything but answering you, and writing to us does not put you on a list.

If you have an account in the investor hall

The hall holds more about you, because it has to know who is reading: your name and e-mail address, your sessions, and which documents you have opened. It carries its own notice saying what it holds and what you can ask for, and that notice is the one that applies to you there.

Who to write to

Write to hello@valotech.org. We answer within thirty days.

When this page changes

We rewrite it rather than adding to the bottom of it, so what you read is what is true now. The date above says when it last changed.
